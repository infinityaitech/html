A historic destination in Ethiopia

Lalibela is a town in the Amhara region of northern Ethiopia. It is widely known for its remarkable rock-hewn churches, which were carved directly into the rock. These historic buildings make Lalibela an important destination for visitors interested in Ethiopian history, culture, and architecture.

What makes Lalibela special?

The churches are known for their unusual construction. Instead of being built by stacking stone blocks, they were cut out of solid rock. The site is valued for its history, religious importance, and distinctive design.

Things visitors can discover

  • Rock-hewn churches and their architecture
  • Local history and cultural traditions
  • Views of the surrounding highlands
  • Local markets, food, and everyday life

Respectful travel

Visitors should respect places of worship, follow local guidance, and ask permission before photographing people. Preserving historic places helps future generations appreciate them too.

Discover the history, respect the culture, and enjoy the journey.
