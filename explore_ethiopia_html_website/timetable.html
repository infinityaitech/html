A Two-Day Visit to Lalibela

Explore Lalibela — Sample Itinerary
Day Time Activity Description
Day 1 8:00–9:00 Breakfast Have breakfast and prepare for the day's visit.
9:00–12:00 Rock-hewn churches Explore Lalibela's famous churches with a local guide.
12:00–1:00 Lunch break Enjoy a traditional Ethiopian meal.
1:00–4:00 Historic sites Continue learning about the history and architecture of Lalibela.
Day 2 8:00–10:00 Local exploration Walk around the town and observe everyday life.
10:00–12:00 Photography Take photographs of the landscape and surrounding highlands.
12:00–2:00 Lunch and departure Have lunch and prepare for the journey home.

Travel Reminder

Plan visits respectfully, follow local guidance, and check current visitor information before travelling.
